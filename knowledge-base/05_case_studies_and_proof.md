# Case Studies and Proof

## Owned Proof: HER ALTAR

HER ALTAR was the founder's owned Shopify brand and testing ground for the Creative Scaling process.

Known proof points:

- 700K+ organic views from owned content
- 7K+ followers in roughly 10 days
- Thousands of site visitors before checkout was interrupted by a payment issue
- The lessons became the Creative Scaling process

## Correct Framing

HER ALTAR should be framed as proof of creative direction, hook testing, organic traction, and rapid audience attention.

It should not be framed as:

- Guaranteed client revenue
- Guaranteed ROAS
- Guaranteed follower growth
- A promise that every brand will replicate the same timeline

## Case Study Narrative

HER ALTAR showed that creative output works best when it is tied to product selection, brand positioning, content strategy, hooks, and structured testing.

The result was not just a set of viral posts. It became the foundation for a repeatable system: research, hooks, concepts, production, delivery, feedback, performance review, and the next sprint.

## How AI Should Use This Proof

Use HER ALTAR when a visitor asks:

- "Have you done this before?"
- "Why should I trust the process?"
- "Does organic content matter?"
- "Can creative strategy create traction?"

Approved response:

"The founder used HER ALTAR as the owned testing ground. It generated 700K+ organic views and 7K+ followers in roughly 10 days. That experience shaped the Creative Scaling process, but it is not a guarantee of the same outcome for every brand."
