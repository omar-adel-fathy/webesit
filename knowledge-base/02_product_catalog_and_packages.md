# Product Catalog and Packages

## Offer Summary

Creative Scaling provides performance creative systems for Shopify brands. The service includes strategy, research, hooks, statics, videos, delivery structure, feedback flow, and performance review.

Creative Scaling supports both:

- Paid creative for channels like Meta and TikTok
- Organic discovery content for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and similar formats

Creative Scaling does not run media buying or manage ad spend.

## Starter Package

Starting price: $2,000 per month

Best for: Shopify brands beginning structured creative testing.

Includes:

- 20 Performance Statics
- Up to 7 Performance Videos
- Creative strategy
- Monthly planning
- 2 revision rounds

Recommended when:

- The brand has demand but inconsistent creative output
- The team needs a clearer testing structure
- The founder wants to move away from random content requests

## Qualification Rules

Best fit:

- Shopify brand
- Usually $30,000+ monthly revenue
- Real product demand
- Active or planned paid testing
- Need for consistent paid and organic assets
- Willing to share brand, product, customer, competitor, and performance context

Weak fit:

- Pre-revenue idea with no product demand
- Looking only for the cheapest editor
- Wants one random video
- Expects guaranteed ad results
- Does not want to share business context
- Needs media buying rather than creative output
