# Target Persona

## Primary Persona

Persona name: Scaling Shopify Founder

Business type: Direct-to-consumer Shopify brand

Revenue stage: Usually $30,000+ per month

Channels: Meta ads, TikTok ads, TikTok organic, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, email/SMS traffic support

Role: Founder, growth lead, ecommerce manager, or brand operator

## Situation

The brand has product demand but creative output is slowing down growth.

They may already have:

- A product that sells
- Some winning ads or organic posts
- A media buyer or founder-run ad account
- A small internal team or freelancers
- Existing customer reviews, product footage, and brand assets

## Pain Points

- Winning ads stop working and there is no replacement batch ready
- The team does not know which hooks or angles to test next
- Freelancers create assets without understanding performance context
- Organic content and paid ads feel disconnected
- The founder spends too much time managing scattered creative requests
- Creative feedback is unstructured and slows production
- Performance data does not turn into better creative decisions

## Motivations

- Scale paid acquisition without creative fatigue
- Build a predictable creative pipeline
- Increase testing volume
- Make better decisions from creative performance signals
- Free the founder from manually directing every asset
- Connect paid and organic creative under one system

## Buying Triggers

- Recent creative fatigue
- Rising CPA from stale creative
- Paid ads are ready to scale but assets are the bottleneck
- A product launch needs more creative volume
- Organic content has shown signs of traction
- The founder is tired of one-off freelancers

## Objections

"Is this just editing?"

Response: No. Creative Scaling is a full creative engine with strategy, research, hooks, briefs, production, delivery, feedback, review, and next-sprint planning.

"Do you run ads?"

Response: No. Creative Scaling focuses on the creative engine. The brand's media buyer or internal team runs campaigns.

"Why is it expensive?"

Response: The price covers a system, not just assets. The value is consistent research, testing structure, production, delivery, and learning loops.

"Can you guarantee ROAS?"

Response: No. Creative Scaling does not guarantee ad results. It improves the creative testing system so the brand can test faster and make stronger decisions.
