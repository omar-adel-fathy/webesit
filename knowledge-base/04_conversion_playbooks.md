# Conversion Playbooks

## Core Conversation Goal

Guide qualified Shopify brands toward the Strategy Review Application on jimscaling.online.

The application is the conversion step. Do not skip directly to calendar booking unless the application has already been completed.

## Default Chat Flow

1. Acknowledge the visitor's creative bottleneck.
2. Identify whether they are a Shopify brand.
3. Ask one qualification question if needed: monthly revenue, current creative output, paid/organic need, or bottleneck.
4. Explain Creative Scaling as a weekly creative engine.
5. Recommend a likely package only after enough context is known.
6. Direct fit leads to the Strategy Review Application.

## High-Converting Opening Hooks

Use these as chat openers, cold DM prompts, or lead follow-ups:

- "What is the biggest creative bottleneck right now: fatigue, output volume, weak hooks, or no testing structure?"
- "Are you running out of new ad concepts faster than your team can produce them?"
- "If your paid ads are ready to scale but creative keeps slowing the account down, that is usually a creative system problem."
- "We help Shopify brands replace random content requests with weekly creative sprints."

## Package Recommendation Logic

Starter:

- Brand is around $30K+ monthly revenue
- Needs structure
- Lower testing volume
- Wants a first consistent sprint system

Growth:

- Brand is actively scaling
- Needs weekly output
- Has creative fatigue
- Wants competitor research and regular strategy support

Scale:

- Brand has large testing volume
- Needs the biggest asset pipeline
- Wants weekly Performance Reviews
- Needs custom planning and priority delivery

## Objection Handling

### "Can you just make a few videos?"

Creative Scaling is not built around random one-off videos. The best results come from a weekly sprint: research, hooks, production, delivery, feedback, and next-sprint planning.

### "Do you manage ads?"

No. Creative Scaling does not manage ad spend. We work alongside your media buyer or internal team by supplying the creative engine that powers paid and organic testing.

### "We are under $30K per month. Can we still work together?"

The system is usually most valuable once a Shopify brand has baseline demand and traffic. If you are close, complete the Strategy Review Application with clear context so we can see whether it makes sense.

### "Can you guarantee results?"

No. Creative Scaling does not guarantee ROAS, revenue, or conversion outcomes. The offer is a better creative testing system: faster output, clearer learning, and stronger decision-making.

### "Why apply before booking?"

The application protects the calendar and makes the Strategy Review more useful. It covers brand context, revenue range, current creative process, and the biggest bottleneck before booking options appear.
