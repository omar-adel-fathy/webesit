# Business Dictionary

## Internal Terminology

Creative Scaling: The brand name and offer. It means a performance creative system for Shopify brands, not a generic scaling agency.

Creative Engine: A repeatable weekly workflow containing research, hooks, creative briefs, production, delivery, feedback, review, and next-sprint planning.

Creative Sprint: A weekly production and learning cycle where the team decides what to make, why it matters, where it will be used, and what it should test.

Performance Statics: Static image ad creatives built to test hooks, offers, products, angles, retargeting, and product storytelling.

Performance Video Creatives: Short-form paid or organic video assets built for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Meta ads, UGC-style tests, and product demos.

Creative Strategy: The planning layer that defines priorities, angles, offers, hooks, and testing hypotheses.

Hook Library: A collection of tested and planned opening ideas, angles, and attention patterns for future creative.

Competitor Research Board: A research workspace tracking competitor ads, offers, product positioning, hooks, landing patterns, and creative trends.

Creative Brief: The document that turns research and strategy into production direction for a specific asset or batch.

Creative Delivery: The organized asset handoff, usually with folders, platform notes, hook labels, CTA notes, and usage context.

Feedback Tracker: The system used to keep revisions, comments, and approvals clear.

Performance Review: A structured review of creative signals, testing outcomes, bottlenecks, and next actions.

Next Sprint Plan: The plan for the following creative sprint based on feedback and performance signals.

Growth Partner: Creative Scaling's operating position. It means the business partners with the brand on creative output and learning, but does not run media buying.

## Industry Jargon

CPA: Cost per acquisition. The cost required to acquire one customer.

ROAS: Return on ad spend. Revenue attributed to ads divided by ad spend.

CTR: Click-through rate. Clicks divided by impressions.

CVR: Conversion rate. Purchases or conversions divided by visitors, sessions, or clicks depending on context.

CPM: Cost per thousand impressions.

UGC: User-generated-content style creative. It may be creator-led or brand-produced, but it should feel native to the platform.

Meta: Facebook and Instagram advertising inventory.

TikTok Creative: Short-form creative built for TikTok paid or organic usage.

Creative Fatigue: The drop in performance that happens when an audience has seen a winning ad too many times or the concept no longer has enough fresh attention.

Creative Testing: The process of testing hooks, angles, formats, offers, product stories, and CTAs to learn what creates stronger performance.

Retargeting Creative: Assets designed for people who already know the brand, viewed a product, visited the site, or engaged with content.

Top-of-Funnel Creative: Assets designed to create first attention and introduce the product or problem.

## Metric Definitions

Monthly Revenue: Gross monthly store revenue, used for fit filtering. Creative Scaling is best suited for Shopify brands usually doing $30K+ per month.

Growth Percentage: The percentage increase from a previous baseline. Formula: ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) * 100.

ROAS: Attributed revenue divided by ad spend. Creative Scaling may discuss ROAS as a client-side performance metric, but does not guarantee ROAS.

Pipeline Velocity: The speed at which creative moves from research to brief, production, delivery, testing, feedback, and next-sprint planning.

Creative Output Volume: The number of statics and videos delivered within a sprint or month.

Testing Volume: The number of distinct creative concepts, hooks, angles, and formats entering a paid or organic test.
